What is ComboMODE?

A fashion tool for generating outfit combinations, organizing your wardrobe and discovering styling ideas.

Does the generator use my wardrobe?

You can enter items you already own and use the wardrobe section to keep your pieces organized.

Can I save outfits?

Yes. Create an account and generated outfits can be saved to your account.

Is the AI stylist real AI?

The current prototype uses built-in styling logic. A production AI API can be connected later.

Are payments active?

Not in this prototype. Payment checkout must be connected to a real payment provider before charging users.

Can I use ComboMODE on mobile?

Yes. The layout includes responsive mobile styling.
