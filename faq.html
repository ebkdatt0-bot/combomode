Everything you need to know about NXTLOOK.

What is NXTLOOK?
NXTLOOK is an AI-powered styling platform that helps you create outfits based on your wardrobe, personal style and the look you're trying to achieve.
How does the AI Stylist work?
Upload or describe your clothing and ask NXTLOOK what to wear. The stylist can help build, rate, improve or completely rethink an outfit.
Is NXTLOOK free?
Yes. NXTLOOK has a free plan with basic styling features. Paid plans unlock additional generations and advanced features.
What is NXTLOOK+?
NXTLOOK+ is the entry-level paid plan. It provides more generations, customization options and additional styling features.
What is the PRO plan?
PRO is the highest NXTLOOK plan and is designed for users who want unlimited generations and advanced styling features.
Can I save my outfits?
Yes. NXTLOOK is designed to let you keep track of your wardrobe and save looks for future outfits.
Can I use NXTLOOK on my phone?
Yes. The website is designed to work across desktop and mobile screens.
Are payments available?
The pricing pages are currently set up, but payment processing still needs to be connected before real purchases can be made.
