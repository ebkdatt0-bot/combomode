FAQ.

Does ComboMODE use my wardrobe?

Yes. The wardrobe feature stores your uploaded pieces in your browser and can send those images to the AI when you build a fit.

Are the prices real?

The current plan prices are the prices displayed on the site. Shop-card product prices are demo placeholders until a real catalog is connected.

Can I use my own clothes?

Yes. Upload your pieces in YOUR WARDROBE and build an outfit from them.
